---
title: Participation at IFAT World University challenge 2022
date: 2022-10-01


---
Participated in IFAT 2022, competing alongside more than 20 international teams from around the world at one of the largest global exhibitions for water, wastewater, waste management, and energy technologies. Our team completed two key tasks as part of the competition:

 - Integrated Water Management Role-Playing Task:** Assumed the roles of technical experts and a town planning officer to evaluate and determine the allocation of water infrastructure projects in a water- and land-scarce town.
 - SIMBA Simulation Task:** Used SIMBA to identify the optimal operation of a water treatment plant under varying operational and demand constraints.
