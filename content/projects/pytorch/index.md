It was a great experience participating in the full-day PyPSA workshop at OTH Regensburg, bringing together researchers from New Zealand, Germany, and Austria. I particularly enjoyed the technical discussions around PyPSA-Earth, including new features such as MGA and CVaR, as well as the opportunity to learn more about contributing to the open-source community. The exchange of ideas on reproducibility, data sharing, and strengthening collaboration between NZ and European research groups was especially valuable. It was also a pleasure to continue these conversations informally over the beautiful Regensburg Christmas market.
